# 00 — Two-Week Meal Plan & Prep Guide

Designed for two adults. Most recipes make four servings so dinner also supplies lunch; batch recipes make six to eight servings and should be frozen in two-serving portions.

## Week 1

- Monday, September 7: Beef Tacos & Black Beans
- Tuesday, September 8: Sheet-Pan Lemon-Herb Chicken, Potatoes & Carrots
- Wednesday, September 9: Leftover Chicken-Feta Chopped Salad & Sourdough
- Thursday, September 10: Spaghetti, Meatballs & Marinara
- Friday, September 11: Dijon Tuna Melts & Cabbage-Cucumber Salad
- Saturday, September 12: Batch Pulled Pork & Roasted Sweet Potatoes
- Sunday, September 13: Pulled-Pork Tacos, Lime Slaw & Crema

## Week 2

- Monday, September 14: Pulled-Pork Fried Rice
- Tuesday, September 15: Sheet-Pan Sausage, Peppers, Onions & Potatoes
- Wednesday, September 16: Batch Lentil-Tomato Soup & Country Bread
- Thursday, September 17: Bacon Carbonara & Green Salad
- Friday, September 18: Soy-Lime Salmon Rice Bowls
- Saturday, September 19: Batch Beef-and-Bean Chili
- Sunday, September 20: Chili-Loaded Baked Potatoes

## Short prep rhythm

- Sunday or Monday: Wash and dry lettuce; shred half a cabbage; chop a few carrots, celery ribs, and bell peppers. Cook rice early enough to chill portions for fried rice.
- Tuesday: Cook extra chicken and reserve about 2 cups before serving.
- Saturday of Week 1: Cook the whole pork shoulder. Refrigerate enough for Sunday and Monday; freeze the rest in two-serving packages.
- Saturday of Week 2: Freeze at least half the chili in two-serving packages.
- Each morning: Check the dinner event for anything that needs thawing.

## Reliable serving estimates

- Mixed meat dishes: 1 to 1 1/4 pounds meat for four servings
- Sheet-pan meat: about 1 1/2 pounds for four servings
- Dry pasta: 6 to 8 ounces for two adults with modest leftovers
- Dry rice: 1 cup for approximately four servings
- Soup or chili: make six to eight servings and freeze half

# 01 — Modular Tacos, Burritos & Bowls

Serves 4 | About 30 minutes | Freezer-friendly filling

## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef, chopped chicken thighs, shredded pork, fish, chorizo, or 2 cups cooked lentils/beans
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced, optional
- 2 teaspoons cumin
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper
- 8 to 10 corn or flour tortillas, or 3 cups cooked rice
- 1 can black or red beans, drained
- Shredded lettuce or cabbage
- Cheddar, cilantro, lime, sour cream, cucumber, or jalapeño for serving

## Method

1. Brown the meat in a wide skillet. If using cooked pork, fish, lentils, or beans, warm them gently instead.
2. Add the onion and pepper; cook until softened. Stir in the seasonings and a splash of water. Simmer until the mixture is moist but not watery.
3. Warm the beans separately with a little cumin and salt.
4. Serve in tortillas or over rice with crunchy vegetables and toppings.

## Easy variations

- Beef: Use ground beef and cheddar.
- Chicken: Finish with lime and cilantro.
- Pork: Crisp shredded pork in a lightly oiled skillet before seasoning.
- Chorizo-potato: Brown chorizo with small diced cooked potatoes.
- Fish: Use cumin, lime, and smoked paprika; cook only until flaky.
- Vegetarian: Combine lentils and black beans, adding tomato paste for richness.

## Lime crema

Mix 1/2 cup sour cream, juice of 1/2 lime, a pinch of salt, and enough water to drizzle. Add minced jalapeño or cilantro if desired.

Freeze only the cooked filling and beans, not tortillas or fresh toppings.

# 02 — Sheet-Pan Chicken or Pork with Vegetables

Serves 4 | 50 minutes | Good for planned leftovers

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds chicken thighs, pork chops, or pork loin pieces
- 1 1/2 pounds yellow or russet potatoes, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3 carrots, sliced thickly
- 1 onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 lemon
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon oregano or Italian seasoning
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the potatoes, carrots, and onion with half the oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 15 minutes.
3. Coat the meat with the remaining oil, garlic, oregano, paprika, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
4. Add the meat to the pan and roast until cooked through: approximately 20 to 30 minutes for boneless chicken thighs or pork pieces, and longer for bone-in thighs. Chicken should reach at least 165°F.
5. Squeeze lemon juice over everything. For the Week 1 plan, reserve about 2 cups of cooked chicken for tomorrow's salad.

## Variations

- Dijon-herb: Add 1 tablespoon Dijon to the meat coating.
- Cajun: Replace oregano with Cajun seasoning and add bell pepper.
- Sausage: Use sliced kielbasa or andouille and add it for the final 20 to 25 minutes.
- Fish: Roast the vegetables first, then add fish for only its final 10 to 15 minutes.

# 03 — Leftover Chicken-Feta Chopped Salad

Serves 2 generously | 15 minutes | Do not freeze

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked chicken, sliced or chopped
- 4 cups lettuce, chopped
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- 1 bell pepper, chopped
- 1 carrot, shredded
- 1/2 cup feta
- Sourdough or country bread

## Dijon vinaigrette

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Shake or whisk the vinaigrette ingredients together.
2. Combine the vegetables, chicken, and feta. Toss with just enough dressing to coat.
3. Serve with toasted bread.

## Variations

Replace the chicken with steak, canned tuna, canned salmon, sausage, chickpeas, or hard-boiled eggs. Parmesan or cheddar can replace feta.

# 04 — Spaghetti, Meatballs & All-Purpose Marinara

Serves 4 | 40 minutes | Sauce and meatballs freeze well

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces spaghetti
- 1 pound frozen meatballs
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 28-ounce can crushed or diced tomatoes, or an equivalent amount of tomato sauce
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Pinch of red pepper flakes, optional
- Parmesan, salt, and pepper

## Method

1. Cook the onion in olive oil until soft. Add the garlic and tomato paste; cook for 1 to 2 minutes.
2. Add tomatoes, Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Simmer for at least 20 minutes.
3. Add the meatballs and simmer until hot throughout.
4. Cook the spaghetti in salted water. Toss with enough sauce to coat and serve with Parmesan.

## Variations

- Sausage and peppers: Brown sliced sausage and bell pepper before building the sauce.
- Lentil Bolognese: Replace meatballs with 2 cups cooked lentils.
- Tuna-tomato: Fold in drained tuna at the end with parsley and lemon.
- Baked pasta: Combine sauce with short pasta or ravioli, cover with cheddar and Parmesan, and bake until bubbling.

Freeze extra sauce in two-serving portions for up to about three months.

# 05 — Dijon Tuna Melts & Crunchy Salad

Serves 2 | 20 minutes | Do not freeze

## Ingredients

- 2 cans tuna, drained
- 1 celery rib, finely diced
- 2 tablespoons sour cream or mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- Black pepper
- 4 slices sourdough or country bread
- 4 slices cheddar, or about 1 cup shredded

## Method

1. Mix the tuna, celery, sour cream, Dijon, lemon, and pepper. Add salt only if needed.
2. Divide between two slices of bread, top with cheddar, and close the sandwiches.
3. Butter the outsides and cook in a covered skillet over medium-low heat until golden and melted. Alternatively, make open-faced melts under the broiler.
4. Serve with shredded cabbage and cucumber tossed in Dijon vinaigrette.

## Variations

Use canned salmon or chicken. Add chopped jalapeño, parsley, cucumber, or bell pepper.

# 06 — Neutral Batch Pulled Pork

Makes 8 servings | 4 to 5 hours mostly unattended | Freezer staple

## Ingredients

- 3 to 4 pounds pork shoulder
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 5 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 cup chicken or vegetable broth
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 2 teaspoons oregano
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 to 2 teaspoons salt, plus pepper
- 1 tablespoon vinegar or lime juice

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 300°F. Put the onion and garlic in a covered Dutch oven or roasting pan.
2. Season the pork with paprika, oregano, cumin, salt, and pepper. Place it over the onions and add the broth.
3. Cover and cook until fork-tender, usually 3 1/2 to 5 hours. Turn once if convenient.
4. Shred the pork, discard large pieces of fat, and mix the meat with enough strained cooking liquid to keep it moist. Add vinegar or lime.
5. For crisp pork, spread a portion in a hot skillet or under the broiler just before serving.

## Use it four ways

- Serve with roasted sweet potatoes and salad.
- Crisp with cumin and lime for tacos.
- Add to fried rice.
- Mix with barbecue sauce for sandwiches or with marinara for a quick ragù.

Refrigerate the next two days' portions promptly. Freeze the remainder in flat two-serving packages with a spoonful of cooking liquid.

# 07 — Lime Slaw

Serves 4 | 10 minutes | Best within 2 days

## Ingredients

- 4 cups finely shredded cabbage
- 1 carrot, shredded
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Salt and pepper
- Minced jalapeño, optional

## Method

Combine everything and massage briefly with clean hands. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.

Use on pork, chicken, beef, or fish tacos; alongside sandwiches; or as the crunchy base of a rice bowl.

# 08 — Leftover Fried Rice

Serves 4 | 25 minutes | Best with cold rice

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cold cooked rice
- 2 cups shredded pork, chicken, steak, or chopped sausage
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 2 cups mixed chopped vegetables such as carrot, peas, corn, pepper, cabbage, or celery
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon vinegar or lime juice
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil or olive oil

## Method

1. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar, and sugar.
2. Heat a wide skillet until hot. Add a little oil, scramble the eggs just until set, and remove them.
3. Add more oil and cook the vegetables until crisp-tender. Add garlic, then the meat.
4. Add cold rice and cook, stirring occasionally, until hot and lightly toasted.
5. Add the sauce and eggs; toss well. Finish with cilantro, lime, or jalapeño if desired.

Avoid using freshly cooked, steamy rice; chilled rice fries rather than turning mushy.

# 09 — Sheet-Pan Sausage, Peppers, Onions & Potatoes

Serves 4 | 45 minutes | Leftovers keep well

## Ingredients

- 12 to 16 ounces kielbasa, andouille, chorizo, or other sausage
- 1 1/2 pounds potatoes, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon vinegar
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss potatoes with half the oil, salt, pepper, and paprika. Roast for 15 minutes.
3. Add sliced sausage, peppers, onion, and remaining oil. Roast 20 to 25 minutes more, stirring once.
4. Stir together Dijon and vinegar and drizzle over the hot pan.

## Variations

- Cajun: Use andouille and Cajun seasoning.
- German-inspired: Use kielbasa, extra Dijon, and cabbage instead of peppers.
- Breakfast hash: Reheat leftovers in a skillet and top with fried eggs.

# 10 — Batch Lentil-Tomato Soup

Makes 6 to 8 servings | About 1 hour | Freezes very well

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 carrots, diced
- 3 celery ribs, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 1/2 cups dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 1 28-ounce can diced tomatoes
- 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon oregano or Italian seasoning
- Salt, pepper, and lemon juice

## Method

1. Cook onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil until softened, 8 to 10 minutes.
2. Add garlic, tomato paste, and spices; cook for 1 minute.
3. Add lentils, tomatoes, and broth. Simmer partially covered until the lentils are tender, usually 30 to 40 minutes.
4. Add water if it becomes too thick. Finish with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

## Variations

- Add browned sausage, bacon, or shredded chicken.
- Stir in chopped cabbage for the final 15 minutes.
- Blend two cups of soup and return it to the pot for a thicker texture.
- Add cream to individual bowls rather than the freezer batch.

Cool promptly and freeze in two-serving portions.

# 11 — Bacon Carbonara

Serves 2 generously | 25 minutes | Eat immediately

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces spaghetti
- 4 ounces bacon, cut into small pieces
- 2 whole eggs plus 1 egg yolk
- 1 cup finely grated Parmesan
- Plenty of black pepper
- Salt
- Up to 1 cup reserved pasta water

## Method

1. Whisk eggs, yolk, Parmesan, and black pepper in a bowl.
2. Cook bacon in a wide skillet until crisp. Turn off the heat, leaving the rendered fat in the pan.
3. Cook spaghetti until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
4. Add hot pasta to the bacon skillet and toss. Let it cool for about 30 seconds so the eggs will not scramble.
5. Add the egg-cheese mixture while tossing constantly. Loosen a spoonful at a time with hot pasta water until glossy and creamy.
6. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and pepper.

Cream is unnecessary, but a small splash can make the technique more forgiving. Add peas if desired.

# 12 — Soy-Lime Salmon Rice Bowls

Serves 4 | 35 minutes | Components keep separately

## Ingredients

- 1 to 1 1/4 pounds salmon fillet, or 2 to 3 cans salmon
- 1 cup dry rice
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 1 carrot, shredded
- 2 cups shredded cabbage or lettuce
- Cilantro and jalapeño, optional
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 teaspoon olive oil

## Method

1. Cook the rice.
2. Mix soy sauce, lime, sugar, garlic, and oil. Reserve half as the bowl dressing.
3. Brush fresh salmon with the other half and roast at 425°F until it flakes easily, usually 10 to 15 minutes depending on thickness. For canned salmon, drain it and gently toss it with a little dressing.
4. Build bowls with rice, salmon, cucumber, carrot, cabbage, and cilantro. Drizzle with the reserved dressing.

## Variations

Use chicken, pork, steak, or a fried egg. Replace soy-lime dressing with lime crema for a richer bowl.

# 13 — Batch Beef-and-Bean Chili

Makes 6 to 8 servings | About 1 hour | Freezes very well

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced, optional
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 2 teaspoons cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1 28-ounce can diced tomatoes
- 1 to 2 cups tomato sauce
- 2 cans black or red beans, drained
- 1 cup beef broth or water, as needed
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Brown the beef in a Dutch oven. Drain excess fat if needed.
2. Add onion, pepper, and jalapeño; cook until softened. Add garlic, tomato paste, and spices and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add tomatoes, tomato sauce, beans, and enough broth for the consistency you prefer.
4. Simmer partially covered for at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Adjust salt and spice.
5. Serve with cheddar, sour cream, cilantro, lime, bread, tortillas, rice, or potatoes.

## Variations

- Replace half the beef with sausage.
- Replace all the beef with an extra can of beans plus 1 cup cooked lentils.
- Add corn or diced sweet potato.

Freeze half in two-serving portions after cooling promptly.

# 14 — Loaded Baked Potatoes or Sweet Potatoes

Serves 2 | 15 minutes active, about 1 hour total | Ideal leftover vehicle

## Ingredients

- 2 very large russet potatoes or sweet potatoes
- About 2 cups hot filling
- Cheddar, sour cream, cilantro, parsley, jalapeño, or chopped cabbage
- Salt, pepper, and olive oil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Scrub and dry the potatoes, prick them several times, rub lightly with oil and salt, and bake directly on a rack until completely tender, usually 45 to 70 minutes.
2. Split and fluff the interiors with salt and pepper.
3. Add hot filling and toppings.

## Filling combinations

- Beef-and-bean chili, cheddar, and sour cream
- Pulled pork, lime slaw, and crema
- Chicken, bacon, cheddar, and broccoli or peas
- Black beans, corn, peppers, cheddar, and cilantro
- Tuna or salmon, Dijon, cucumber, and cheddar

# 15 — Kitchen-Rescue Frittata

Serves 4 | 35 minutes | Good warm or at room temperature

## Ingredients

- 8 eggs
- 1/3 cup milk or cream
- 1 cup cooked potatoes or roasted vegetables
- 1 cup cooked meat, optional
- 1 cup cheddar, Parmesan, or feta
- 1 tablespoon butter or olive oil
- Salt, pepper, and herbs

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Whisk eggs with milk, a modest amount of salt, and pepper.
3. Heat an oven-safe skillet, add butter, and warm the cooked filling.
4. Pour in the eggs and scatter cheese over the top. Cook without stirring for 2 minutes.
5. Transfer to the oven and bake until just set in the center, about 12 to 18 minutes.

Good combinations include bacon-potato-cheddar, sausage-pepper, chicken-feta, and sweet potato-black bean.
