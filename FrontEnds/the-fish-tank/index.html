01 / THE STUDIO

A company with
an explorer’s spirit.

The Fish Tank LLC develops and publishes games and software, with Fathom Fall at the heart of our current work.

Based in Fairfax, Vermont and led by Seth Lunn, we bring together software development, game design, and a love of discovery. Our work on Fathom Fall explores aquatic worlds, party building, and the choices that make each dungeon run your own.
